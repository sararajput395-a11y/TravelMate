About TravelMate 🧸

A soft, pastel haven created for lovers of notebooks, stickers, and joyful travels.

💖

Why We Made TravelMate

Traditional travel apps feel like corporate spreadsheets: stiff calendars, cold barcodes, and rigid tables. We believe trip planning should feel like sitting at a sunlit cafe table with your favorite colored gel pens, washi tape rolls, and polaroid snaps.

Whether you are solo backpacking through Kyoto bamboo groves, road-tripping with friends along the Amalfi cliffs, or packing for a quiet seaside escape, TravelMate turns every logistical detail into a warm keepsake memory.

🎨
Pastel Scrapbook Philosophy

Designed with warm cream paper, taped polaroids, playful doodles, and stress-free rounded cards.

⚡
Zero Clutter, 100% Client-Side

Runs entirely in your browser with lightweight HTML, CSS & vanilla JS. Fast, private and responsive.

🧳
Complete Companion

From day-by-day morning/afternoon/evening flows to suitcase checklists and piggy bank monitoring.
